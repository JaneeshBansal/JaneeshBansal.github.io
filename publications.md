## Research outputs

My research focuses on extracting reliable demographic and epidemiological signal from malaria parasite genomes.

I am currently preparing publication records and project notes for this section. In the meantime, the main research themes are:

* Bayesian inference for pathogen population history.
* Deep learning and simulation-based approaches to demographic inference.
* Distinguishing population bottlenecks from substructure.
* Approximate Bayesian computation and the interpretation of hmmIBD results.
